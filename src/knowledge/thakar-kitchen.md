## FAQs
- What is Retort Technology? The same process used to make food for astronauts and the Indian
  Army. The food is sealed in a pouch and cooked at high heat and pressure, killing all bacteria
  naturally. No preservatives needed. The result is shelf-stable food that tastes exactly like
  freshly made.
- No preservatives - how does it last 12 months? Because of Retort Technology. The high-heat
  sterilisation eliminates all microorganisms inside the sealed pouch. As long as the seal is
  intact, nothing can spoil it - no preservatives, no chemicals.
- How is it different from dehydrated food? Dehydrated food removes all moisture - you add water
  and cook it. Thakar Kitchen is fully cooked, ready food: open the pouch, heat for 3 minutes, eat.
  Taste, texture and nutrition are preserved.
- How do I heat it? Two ways: drop the sealed pouch in boiling water for 3-4 minutes, or cut it
  open and heat in a pan for 2-3 minutes on medium flame. No microwave needed.
- Is it safe for kids and elderly? Yes. No artificial preservatives, no added colours, made with
  100% groundnut oil. The ingredients are the same ones used in any Indian home kitchen.
- Do I need to refrigerate it? No. Store in a cool dry place (shelf, cupboard, bag). No fridge
  needed until you open the pouch. Once opened, consume immediately.
- Can I carry it while travelling or on flights? Yes. Lightweight, no spill risk, no refrigeration
  needed - trains, road trips, flights, hostels.
